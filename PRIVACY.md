# Shhpoiler Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Shhpoiler does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device.

- **Where it runs:** Only on x.com, twitter.com, instagram.com, youtube.com (including m.youtube.com and YouTube players embedded in other pages) and reddit.com (including old.reddit.com and new.reddit.com).
- **What it reads:** On those sites, Shhpoiler reads the page on your device to decide what to cover: the text of posts, comments and Instagram messages, account and community names, and the addresses of pictures and videos. This never leaves your browser.
- **What it remembers while a tab is open:** Which pictures, videos and posts you uncovered, so they stay uncovered while you scroll. This is kept in the tab's memory only and is gone when you close or reload the tab.
- **What it saves:** Your Shhpoiler settings (on/off, sites, keyword packs, your own keywords, trusted and hidden accounts, display options). They are stored with Chrome's built-in `storage.sync`, which syncs only to your own Google account if you have Chrome Sync on. You can delete them at any time by removing the extension.
- **Network:** Shhpoiler makes no network requests and has no servers, analytics or tracking.
- **Third parties:** None.
- **Chrome Web Store User Data Policy:** The use of information received by Shhpoiler adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Page content is used only for the user-facing feature of hiding spoilers, and is never stored, transferred, used for ads or sold.
